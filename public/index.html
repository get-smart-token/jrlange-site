01 / ABOUT

I've founded three universities on three continents and worked with AI in education since 2006.

I hold an Ed.D. from the University of Exeter, an M.Ed. from the University of Reading and an M.A. from the London School of Economics. I'm the founder and president of the Digital Financial Aid Corporation, a Florida 501(c)(3), and I consult on AI automation and edtech. Based in St. Petersburg, Florida, with deep ties to Vienna.

Through all of it, the same question: who decides what counts as knowledge, who gets access to it, and what happens when machines take over that role?

  1. 2012 · TEDx

    The optimist

    At TEDx Nazarbayev University I asked "Who owns knowledge?" and "Who is the digital Socrates?", and described the rise of the digitized public intellectual.

  2. 2015 · Interchange

    The warning

    My paper argued that the professor was becoming "a cog, a manager, in principle replaceable by management software."

  3. 2023–2024 · The novel

    The story

    I wrote THE AITHORITY, a satire of AI-run schooling, attention and control. Agents passed. A first edition was printed in May 2024.

THE AITHORITY

At the Alcademy, a benevolent AI runs the schedule, the grades, the moods and the minutes of gratitude. Two teens, They and Daisy, start thinking for themselves. Satire, romance and a DJ battle against a machine, written in invented language built to be hard for AI to read.
